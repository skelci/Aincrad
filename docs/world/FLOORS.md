# Aincrad Floor System

## 1. Overview

Aincrad consists of **100 floors**, each a self-contained level with a distinct theme, difficulty tier, and boss. Floors are grouped into **10 tiers of 10 floors**. Players progress by defeating each floor's boss, which unlocks the stairway/portal to the next floor.

## 4. Difficulty Scaling Formulas

All formulas use `floor` (1–100) and the mob's tier-1 base stats.

### 4.1 Mob Health

```
HP(floor) = base_HP × (1 + 0.22 × (floor − 1))
```

- Floor 1: 1.00× base
- Floor 50: 11.78× base
- Floor 100: 22.78× base

### 4.2 Mob Damage

```
Damage(floor) = base_damage × (1 + 0.18 × (floor − 1))
```

- Floor 1: 1.00× base
- Floor 50: 9.82× base
- Floor 100: 18.82× base

### 4.3 XP Reward

```
XP(floor) = base_XP × (1 + 0.15 × (floor − 1))
```

- Floor 1: 1.00× base
- Floor 50: 8.35× base
- Floor 100: 15.85× base

### 4.4 Mob Spawn Count

```
spawn_count(floor) = base_count × (1 + 0.10 × (floor − 1))
```

- Floor 1: 1.00× base
- Floor 100: 10.90× base

### 4.5 Boss Stats

Bosses use the same formulas with a boss multiplier:

```
Boss_HP(floor) = base_HP × (1 + 0.22 × (floor − 1)) × 8
Boss_Damage(floor) = base_damage × (1 + 0.18 × (floor − 1)) × 3
Boss_XP(floor) = base_XP × (1 + 0.15 × (floor − 1)) × 10
```

### 4.6 Loot Quality

```
loot_tier(floor) = floor(floor / 10)   # 0–10
```

Higher loot tiers unlock better enchantments, rarer materials, and higher-tier equipment drops.

## 5. Floor Size and Layout

- Each floor is a **512 × 512 block** area (32 × 32 chunks), enclosed by bedrock walls.
- Terrain height varies by theme (flat for plains, mountainous for cave, etc.), typically 20–40 blocks of vertical variation.
- The **boss arena** is generated at the floor's center (or farthest point from spawn), a circular arena of **radius 40 blocks** with a distinct visual boundary (e.g., a ring of themed blocks).
- The **spawn point** is at the floor's edge, near the entrance portal from the previous floor.

## 6. Boss Arena Generation

- The arena is a flat or gently domed circle, radius 40 blocks.
- A ring of themed blocks (e.g., obsidian for lava, ice for ice) marks the boundary.
- The boss spawns at the arena center when a player enters the ring.
- During the fight, a barrier prevents leaving the arena (optional; can be configured).
- On boss death, the arena center activates the **stairway/portal** to the next floor.

## 7. Stairway / Teleport to Next Floor

- After the boss is defeated, a **portal frame** appears at the arena center (or the existing stairway activates).
- Entering the portal teleports the player to the next floor's spawn point.
- The portal is a custom block/entity with a themed particle effect.
- If the player dies before entering, the boss remains defeated and the portal stays active.

## 8. Floor Unlock Progression

- Each player (or guild) has a **highest unlocked floor**.
- Defeating a floor's boss unlocks the next floor for the player.
- Progress is stored per-player in the mod's save data and synced to the plugin for leaderboards.
- Players can revisit any unlocked floor.
- The Floor 1 plaza is always unlocked and serves as the hub.

## 9. Safe Zones

- Each floor's spawn area (a 32 × 32 block zone around the entrance) is a **safe zone**: no mob spawning, no PvP.
- The Floor 1 plaza is a larger safe zone (the main town).
- Safe zones are visually marked (e.g., a change in floor texture or a particle boundary).
